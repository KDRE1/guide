# What's new in the REACH Engine

Newest version first. Written for students: plain words, 2 to 4 lines per version.
This is the only copy to edit. `build.sh` copies it into the update skill.

## 0.2.5

- **Saving your notes:** you now save your notes once, when you finish your first step (Ready), so you can dive right in. Your guide asks if you're on a computer or your phone, then walks you through it, step by step.

## 0.2.4

- **Updating without losing your place:** before you update, type **I want to start a new chat. Can I have my notes?** in your REACH chat, and add the file to your project. If a new chat ever seems a step behind, your guide shows you how to bring your newest notes over, so nothing gets redone.

## 0.2.3

- **Starting a new chat?** Type **Keep going with my REACH**, so your REACH guide answers (handy if you have the DREAM Engine too). In the same chat, **Keep going** is all you need.
- **On free Claude with the DREAM Engine too?** Your guide shows you one line to change in your project, so each guide answers in its own chat.

## 0.2.2

- **Remake a viral Reel:** send your guide the link to a Reel that's taking off in your niche, and it checks it's really a winner, finds why it works, and remakes it for your topic and your buyer (with Apify connected, it can even read what's said in it). Type **Remake this Reel for my niche** and paste the link.
- **Need a hand?** Type **I need more support** anytime, and your guide helps with what's going on right then.

## 0.2.1

- **Stay in one chat:** keep going in the same chat from step to step. Your guide gives you your notes only when you want a new chat, so there's less to swap.
- **Your roadmap always matches your notes,** and shows only the parts you'll actually do.

## 0.2.0

- **More of Kristin's findings in every step:** checking a post against that account's usual views when you study, Reel timing and where text sits on screen, carousels people screenshot and send, which Story sticker to use, comments that get noticed, turning one long video into a week of posts, and replying to a question with a Reel.
- **A sounds-human check** on everything your guide writes for you, before you see it.

## 0.1.0

- **Welcome to the REACH Engine!** Your guide takes you from a brand new account (or the page you already have) to a full content system in five steps: **R**eady your profile, **E**xplore your buyer's words and what works, **A**ttract with posts that stop the scroll, **C**onnect every week, and **H**elp them buy.
- **Your guide can look at Instagram for you** once you connect Apify (free to start): it checks your profile like a visitor would and studies what works in your niche.
- **Feeling stuck?** Type **I'm stuck** for a short list of things you can ask.
- **Honest feedback:** your guide tells you kindly when a post or plan won't work, and why, based on Kristin's method. It won't just agree with you.
