## 0.1.0

- **Welcome to the REACH Engine!** Your guide takes you from a brand new account (or the page you already have) to a full content system in five steps: **R**eady your profile, **E**xplore your buyer's words and what works, **A**ttract with posts that stop the scroll, **C**onnect every week, and **H**elp them buy.
- **Your guide can look at Instagram for you** once you connect Apify (free to start): it checks your profile like a visitor would and studies what works in your niche.
- **Feeling stuck?** Type **I'm stuck** for a short list of things you can ask.
- **Honest feedback:** your guide tells you kindly when a post or plan won't work, and why, based on Kristin's method. It won't just agree with you.
