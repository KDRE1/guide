# When they're stuck: things they can say

Read this when they say they're stuck, lost or overwhelmed, "I don't know what to post", or ask "what can I ask you?", "what can you do?" or "help".
**Show only the part for where they are** (their current stage, plus "Anytime"), as a short list they can tap or copy. Never the whole list at once. Then end with one clear next step.
Kristin also gives students this list as a printable, so the wording here matches it. Keep the phrases exactly as written.

> **Feeling stuck? That's normal.** Here are a few things you can say to me right now:
> {the lines for their stage}
> Or just tell me what's going on, in your own words. That works too.

---

## Anytime

- **"Start my REACH Engine"**: starts your social media guide
- **"Keep going"**: picks up exactly where you left off
- **"Where am I?"**: shows your REACH roadmap
- **"/update"**: shows what's new in the REACH Engine
- **"I'm stuck"**: shows this list
- **"I'm busy this week. What's the smallest thing I can post?"**
- **"I'm nervous about posting. Help me start small."**
- **"Explain that more simply."** or **"Give me an example."**

## Platform and profile

- **"Which platform should I start on?"**
- **"Should I start a new account or keep mine?"**
- **"Give me handle ideas."**
- **"Write my bio."**
- **"Design my highlight covers."**
- **"Help me pick my look."** (your fonts, colors and your thing)
- **"Connect Apify."** (so your guide can look at Instagram for you)
- **"Check my profile."**

## Pillars and ideas

- **"Check my buyer research for gaps."**
- **"What are my core topics?"**
- **"What are my content pillars?"**
- **"I don't know what to post."**
- **"Give me 10 post ideas for this week."**
- **"Turn this into a post: {something that happened today}."**
- **"Make my content grid."**

## Study what works

- **"Study these accounts: {@account}, {@account}, {@account}."**
- **"What's working in my niche right now?"**
- **"Find my best posts."** (if you already have a page)

## Your posts

- **"Write a Reel script about {topic}."**
- **"Give me 3 hooks for this."**
- **"Make this hook more specific."**
- **"Write a post that gives away my freebie."**
- **"Write my caption."**
- **"What text should go on screen?"**
- **"Make a carousel about {topic} in my Canva."**
- **"Plan my Stories for today."**
- **"I don't want to show my face. What can I post?"**

## Your rhythm

- **"What should I post today?"**
- **"Make my content pack for this week."**
- **"Plan my next 30 days."**
- **"Help me batch my posts."**

## Selling

- **"When can I start selling?"**
- **"Write a sales post."**
- **"Set up a comment keyword for my link."**
- **"Write my reply to this message:"** (then paste it)
- **"Plan my launch."**
- **"Help me plan a live to sell my product."**

## Growing

- **"Check-in time."**
- **"Here are my numbers."** (then add a screenshot)
- **"Why aren't my posts getting views?"**
- **"What were my best posts this month?"**
- **"Remake my best post."**
- **"Should I add YouTube?"**
