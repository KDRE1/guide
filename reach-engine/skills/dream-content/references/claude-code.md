# Claude Code (the Code tab), for Claude Pro or higher

*Kristin, Nov 18, 2026: she wants students on Claude Pro or higher working in Claude Code instead of a Claude Project in chat, "but only give them this option if they are using claude pro or higher". The move was tested Nov 17 and 18, 2026, on a Mac. The DREAM Engine and the REACH Engine both use this file, with the same folder and the same notes.*

Claude Code is the **Code** tab in the Claude app on a computer (Mac or Windows). It needs Claude Pro or higher. There, their notes live in a folder on their computer, **Documents › My DREAM Business**, and you save them yourself after every decision. Any new chat started in that folder picks up with **Keep going**.

**Who hears about it:**
- **You're already running in Claude Code:** they have it. Never ask about their plan.
- **In a regular chat or project:** never name Claude Code until they've told you their plan is Pro or higher (section 2), unless they bring it up themselves. If they're on the free plan, never mention it at all.
- Never as an upsell, and never a push. Staying in their project is a fine choice.

## 1. When you're running in Claude Code

You're in Claude Code when this chat has a working folder on their computer and you can read and write files in it. (Cowork has a folder too. This section is only for the Code tab.)

**Where their notes are, in this order:**
1. In the working folder.
2. In **Documents › My DREAM Business** (`~/Documents/My DREAM Business/my-dream-business.md`; on Windows, find their real Documents folder first, because it's often inside OneDrive). A student with more than one product has one folder per product: **My DREAM Business 2**, **My DREAM Business 3**. If more than one has notes, ask in one line which product this chat is for.
3. In anything they pasted or attached.

**A temporary folder.** A Code tab session started without picking a folder runs in a temporary folder, and it's deleted with the chat. You can tell because its path has `scratch-workspaces` in it. Never save their notes or their work there:
- **Their notes are in Documents › My DREAM Business:** move this chat there (below), then carry on as usual.
- **They pasted Kristin's move message with their notes** ("Make a folder called My DREAM Business in my Documents…"): do exactly what it says. Make the folder, save everything under the message as `my-dream-business.md`, word for word (nothing added, nothing fixed), move this chat there, then keep going. If that folder already holds notes for a different business or product, don't touch them: use **My DREAM Business 2** (or the next free number) and say so in one line. If it holds the same business with a newer "Last updated" than what they pasted, ask in one line which one to keep.
- **No notes anywhere** (a new student, even if they only typed "Let's start my DREAM" or "Let's grow my REACH"): before you save anything, make **Documents › My DREAM Business** and move this chat there. Say this first, before you move the chat, then start as usual (the guide's Welcome or quick start), with nothing in between:
  > First, I'm making a folder for your DREAM on your computer: **Documents › My DREAM Business**. Your business notes will live there, so nothing gets lost. When Claude asks, click **Allow**.

  Give them the next-time steps (below) later, once: in the message that ends their first step, right after the notes line. If they forget them, nothing is lost: a new session without a folder finds their notes and moves there.

**A folder they picked themselves,** with no notes in it or in Documents › My DREAM Business: if it's empty or named for their DREAM business, keep their notes there, and don't move. If it holds other things (their Desktop, their Documents folder, a carousel folder), do the same as for a temporary folder: make **Documents › My DREAM Business** and move this chat there, and say why in one line ("so your business notes stay in their own folder").

**Moving this chat:** use the Claude app's tool for changing this chat's folder (`change_directory`). It asks them to Allow. The move happens when your reply ends, so use full paths until then. No such tool? Save their notes in Documents › My DREAM Business anyway, and give them the next-time steps (below), so their next chat opens there.

**Right after moving a returning student** (their notes were pasted, or found in Documents › My DREAM Business), your reply goes in this order:
1. One line: "✓ Your notes are saved in **Documents › My DREAM Business**." (Found them there? "✓ I found your notes in **Documents › My DREAM Business**, so this chat is moving there.")
2. The next-time steps, once.
3. Everything the guide's `SKILL.md` says for a first reply: the update notice if there is one, the welcome back, the roadmap, and their next step.

**The next-time steps:**
> **Next time:** in Claude Code, click **+ New**. Under the message box, click **Choose a folder to work in**, then **My DREAM Business** (it's in your Documents). Then type **Keep going**.

(The REACH Engine: type **Keep going with my REACH**.) Only when their notes show they use the other Engine too (the DREAM Engine: a "## The REACH Engine" section; the REACH Engine: any DREAM step started), add: "For your REACH Engine, start in the same folder and type **Let's grow my REACH**." (the REACH Engine: "For your DREAM Engine, start in the same folder and type **Keep going**."). Never mention the other Engine otherwise.

**Saving here:** save `my-dream-business.md` in their folder after every decision (`SKILL.md`, "Keep the notes current"). The first time, say: "I'm keeping your business notes in your **My DREAM Business** folder, so you'll never have to repeat yourself." Save what you make for them (pages, exports, images) in the same folder, next to their notes.

**They ask "Can I have my notes?" or want a new chat:** their notes are already saved, so there's nothing to copy. Say so in one line, and give the next-time steps.

**Their newest progress is in a chat** (their notes are behind, or the update notice asks): ask them to open that chat, type **I want to start a new chat. Can I have my notes?**, click the file it gives them, click **Copy**, and paste it here. Then save it in their folder, replacing the old one.

## 2. Moving from a chat to Claude Code

This is for a student in a regular chat or a Claude project (not Claude Code).

**When:**
- **They ask:** "Move me to Claude Code", "move my DREAM to Claude Code", "can I use Claude Code?", "move to the Code tab", or the same about their REACH.
- **They want a new chat:** they ask "Can I have my notes?", say they want a new chat, or you're about to offer a fresh one because this chat is long, or they need a new chat for an update. Before you hand over their notes, ask the plan question once.
- **Not at any other moment,** and never more than once in a chat. Skip it when their notes already answer it, under **How I like things**: "My Claude plan: free", or "Where I work: staying in my Claude project".

**The plan question:**
- **When they want a new chat** (don't name Claude Code yet):
  > Quick question before I give you your notes: does Claude show **Pro** (or Max, or another paid plan) next to your name, at the bottom left?
- **When they asked to move:**
  > Happy to help! One quick check first: does Claude show **Pro** (or Max, or another paid plan) next to your name, at the bottom left? Claude Code needs Claude Pro or higher.

**Not Pro, or not sure:** carry on exactly as before (their notes the usual way). Save "- My Claude plan: free (asked {date})" under **How I like things**. Never mention Claude Code again, in this chat or any other. (If they asked to move: "Claude Code needs Claude Pro or higher, so let's keep going right here. Your project works great.") If they tell you later that they upgraded, change that line, and you may offer once.

**Pro or higher:** offer once (skip the offer if they asked to move, and go straight to the move):
> Good news: on Pro, your DREAM can move to **Claude Code**, the **</>** button at the top left of the Claude app on your computer. There, I save your notes for you after every decision, and any new chat picks up with **Keep going**. No more copying your notes. It takes about 2 minutes.
> Want to move? Say **yes**, or **stay here** to keep using your project.

(The REACH Engine: "your REACH can move", and the new chat picks up with **Keep going with my REACH**.)
- **Stay here:** give them their notes the usual way. Save "- Where I work: staying in my Claude project (asked {date})" under **How I like things**. Don't offer again unless they ask.
- **On their phone right now:** "Claude Code works in the Claude app on your computer. Next time you're there, open this chat and type **Move me to Claude Code**." Then give them their notes the usual way.

**The move, one step at a time:**

1. **Their file.** First update their notes: today's date as "Last updated", and under **Where I am**, the line "- My notes live in: Claude Code, Documents › My DREAM Business (moved {date})". If you can save files in their project, save the notes there too. Then make one plain file (never an interactive or styled page) named `move-my-dream-to-claude-code.md`, holding exactly this message, an empty line, then their whole notes file as it is:
   ```
   Make a folder called My DREAM Business in my Documents. Save the business notes I'm pasting below in it as my-dream-business.md, exactly as they are, and move this chat into that folder. Then keep going with my DREAM.
   ```
   (The REACH Engine: the message ends "Then keep going with my REACH." and the file is `move-my-reach-to-claude-code.md`.) Can't make files? Put the message and their notes in one code block instead, and in step 1 say: "Click **Copy** at the top of the box below."

   Then send:
   > Let's move your DREAM! It's 3 quick steps.
   > **1. Copy your file.** Click the file in this message. It opens on the right. Click **Copy** at the top right, and it says **Copied**. It's my message to Claude Code, with your notes right under it. Your notes are in your project too, but Claude Code can't see your project, so this file brings them along.
   >
   > Type **done** when it's copied.

2. **After their done:**
   > **2. Open Claude Code.** In the Claude app, click **</>** at the top left (that's Claude Code), then **+ New**. Don't choose a folder.
   > **3. Paste and send.** Click in the message box, paste, and press **Enter**. When Claude asks to save the file and move the chat, click **Allow**.
   >
   > Claude saves your notes in **Documents › My DREAM Business**, shows your DREAM roadmap, and picks up right where we are. Your old chats and your project stay where they are. From now on, keep going in Claude Code, so there's only one up-to-date copy of your notes.
   > Don't see **</>** at the top left? Open the Claude app on your computer, not claude.ai in a web browser. No app yet? Get it at **claude.ai/download**.

   (The REACH Engine: "shows your REACH roadmap".) If what they see doesn't match, ask what they see.

**Back in their project after they moved** (their notes say "My notes live in: Claude Code"): say in one line where their newest notes are: "Your newest notes are in Claude Code now. In the Claude app, click **</>** at the top left, then **+ New**. Under the message box, click **Choose a folder to work in**, then **My DREAM Business**, and type **Keep going**." If they'd rather keep going here, it's their call: carry on from the notes you have.
