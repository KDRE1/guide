# Hooks: the first line that stops the scroll

Read this whenever you write an opening line for a student: a video or Reel opener, a caption's first line, a pin's headline, a community post, a launch message, or a Story's first slide. Email subject lines follow their own rules (`market-places.md`, section 3) but can borrow these shapes.
This is your reference for Kristin's hooks (growth, nurture and sales hooks). **Students don't need to see this file.** You use it to write hooks for them, in their words.

---

## How to write hooks for a student

1. **Start from their notes.** Their buyer's own words, struggles, wants and questions (Research), their marketing map and message bank (Market), and their tone.
2. **Pick the job:**
   - **Growth:** reach new people. Teach, compare, bust a myth.
   - **Nurture:** build trust with people who know them. Story, day in the life, behind the scenes.
   - **Sales:** help ready people take the next step. How it works, what's inside, real results.
3. **Pick 3 formulas** below that fit the job and the format, and fill every blank with something real and specific to them.
4. **Make it short.** On screen: 12 words or fewer (Kristin's Reel checklist). Then rewrite it in half the words, twice, and add back only what's needed (Kristin's half-words exercise).
5. **Check it like their buyer:** would *their buyer* stop scrolling for this? It doesn't matter whether the student or you would. If not, rewrite it.
6. **Offer 3 options,** with the job and format for each. They pick.

**Fast ways to find a hook** (Kristin's findings):
- **Their buyer's questions are hooks.** Turn a real question or comment into a line: "Where does all our money go?" becomes "If your money is gone before payday, try this."
- **Study what's working,** then make it theirs: keep the shape of a hook from a big account in their niche, and change every word to fit their buyer.
- **Picture their buyer right now,** at the moment they read it: where they are, what's hard, then the result without the stress of getting there.

## The shapes behind every hook

**Three structures** (Kristin's findings):
- **If-then:** "If you're {situation}, {do this / here's why}."
- **Question:** "Why is it so hard to {result}?"
- **Statement:** "Most {who} don't realize {truth}."

**Every hook opens with one of these** (and ideally names the buyer, at least indirectly):
- **A call-out:** names who it's for ("Moms on a tight budget…")
- **A pain:** names what's hard right now
- **A dream:** paints the result they want, in something they can see or count
- **Curiosity:** opens a small gap they want closed, then the post closes it

**Fear and opportunity** (Kristin's findings): people stop for a threat or an opportunity.
- A fear hook must come with the fix. Keep it gentle for this audience: a common mistake, never scare tactics.
- An opportunity hook names a result they can see or count ("every bill paid on time", "a month planned in 15 minutes"). Vague results ("feel better") get scrolled past.
- **Numbers help, but only true ones.** Time saved, steps, pages, days. Never invented income, weight or follower numbers.

## Kristin's 7 hook types

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Education** | It took me {time} to learn this. I'll teach it to you in 60 seconds. |
| **Storytelling** | I started {thing} at {age or moment} with {nothing / one idea}. Here's what happened. |
| **Comparison** | This {option A} vs. this {option B}: one gets you {result}, one doesn't. |
| **Myth busting** | You've been doing {thing} wrong this whole time. Here's what actually works. |
| **Authority** | How I went from {before} to {after} in {time}: the exact steps. *(Only if it's true and theirs.)* |
| **Day in the life** | Day in the life of a {who} who {does what they do}. |
| **Pattern interrupt** | I don't believe in {common belief}, and here's why that changed everything. |

## Growth hooks (reach new people)

**Teach**
- It took me {time} to figure this out. Here it is in 60 seconds.
- Here's exactly how to {result}, step by step.
- {Number} ways to {result} without {what they dread}.
- If you're a {who} and you want {result}, save this.
- Give me 60 seconds and I'll show you how to {result}.
- The easiest way to {result}, even if {obstacle}.
- {Topic} 101, for {who} who are just starting.
- If I had to start {topic} over from scratch, here's exactly what I'd do.
- If I woke up {pain} tomorrow and wanted {result} by {time}, here's my plan.
- {Number} things every {who} should know about {topic}.
- Did you know you can {result} in just {time}?
- Here's the order you should {do the task} in.

**Mistakes**
- The {number} mistakes I see most when {who} {do the task}.
- Stop {doing X}. Do this instead.
- You're not bad at {thing}. Nobody ever showed you how.
- If you {do X this way}, here's why it's not working.
- Don't {do X} until you've done this one thing.
- {Number} things I'd never do as a {who}.

**Myth busting**
- Everyone says {common belief}. Here's what actually works.
- {Common belief} won't get you {result}. Here's why.
- I don't believe in {common belief}. I believe in {their belief}.
- More {who} need to hear this: {truth}.
- {Thing} is easier than you think.

**Comparison**
- This vs. this: one gets you {result}, and one doesn't.
- What {who} think {thing} looks like vs. what it really looks like.
- Cheap vs. expensive {thing}: is it worth it?
- This is {thing} before {action}. This is after.
- {Option A} or {option B} for {result}? I tried both.

**Curiosity and pattern interrupt**
- I think I just found the easiest {topic} shortcut.
- Nobody talks about this part of {topic}.
- Why does nobody talk about {pain}?
- This sounds strange, but it works: {tip}.
- Here's what's really going on when {pain}.

**Call-outs**
- {Who}? This one's for you.
- If {specific situation}, watch this before {moment}.
- To every {who} who {struggle}: you're not alone, and here's what helped.

**Lists**
- {Number} {things} that save me {time} every week.
- Every {thing} explained in one sentence.
- The only {number} {things} you need to {result}.

## Nurture hooks (build trust)

**Story**
- I didn't always {good habit}. Here's what changed.
- If you'd told {younger them} I'd {now}, I wouldn't have believed you.
- Okay, real talk: I wasn't always this {trait}.
- This is the moment everything changed for me with {topic}.
- {Time} ago I was {before}. Here's what I learned.
- I have a confession: {honest truth}.
- My biggest mistake when I started {thing}.
- Here's what nobody tells you about {starting thing}.
- Why I started {their business / their product}.
- What I wish I'd known before {milestone}.

**Day in the life and behind the scenes**
- Day {number} of {their journey or goal}.
- Welcome back to {series name}, where I {what they do}.
- A day in my life as a {who} who {does their thing}.
- Come {do the thing} with me.
- What my mornings look like while {situation}.
- You know those days when {relatable moment}? Today was one.
- Behind the scenes of making my {product}.
- I tried {thing} for {time}. Here's what happened.

**Conversation**
- The question I get asked most: {question}.
- Some of you asked how I {thing}. Here's the honest answer.
- The {thing} I do every single day, and why.
- My {routine} as a busy {who}.

## Sales hooks (help ready people take the next step)

Real proof only. A result, a before and after, or a customer's words must be true, theirs (or shared with permission), and typical, never a promise (`rules.md`, rules 3 and 7).

- How to turn this into this in {number} simple steps.
- My {thing} used to look like this. Now it looks like this.
- Here's exactly how my {product} works.
- A peek inside my {product}.
- If {pain} stresses you out, here's how my {product} helps.
- {Number} things inside my {product}, and what each one does for you.
- Why I made {product} for {who}.
- This is for you if {situation}. It's not for you if {situation}.
- Everything you need to {result}, in one {format}.
- You asked, so I made it: {product}.
- What you get when you {get / download} {product}.
- {Objection}? Here's what I'd tell you.
- The things I wish I'd had before {milestone}. So I made them.
- {Customer's first name} {got result} without {pain}. *(Real, with permission.)*
- I {real result} in {time}. Here's what I'd do if I started over. *(Only if it's true and theirs.)*

## Never

- **Invented results or numbers:** income, weight, followers, "sold out", "bestseller" (`rules.md`, rules 1, 3 and 7).
- **Health, weight-loss or money promises,** even as a "hook".
- **Fear without a fix,** shaming the reader, or fake urgency ("before it's gone").
- **Attacking a brand or person** ("exposing {brand}"), or anything a platform would treat as harmful or dangerous.
- **Swearing or crude words,** unless it's genuinely how the student talks and they ask for it.
- **Copying a creator's hook word for word.** Keep the shape, change the words.
